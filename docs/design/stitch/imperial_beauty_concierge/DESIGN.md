## Brand & Style

The design system embodies the prestige, pampering, and precision of a high-end beauty, wellness, and salon concierge. Rooted in an aesthetic of modern luxury, the visual style combines deep, aristocratic plum-violets with gentle lavender washes and warm, creamy undertones. The interface delivers an atmosphere that feels tactile, serene, and exquisitely curated—reminiscent of stepping into a boutique spa lounge.

Targeted at discerning clients seeking haircare, nail artistry, massages, and esthetic treatments, the experience balances visual inspiration (curated salon stories, service portfolios, and artisan spotlights) with instantaneous operational utility (fluid geolocation maps, seamless booking flows, and unambiguous time-slot scheduling).

The overarching aesthetic blends **Contemporary Minimalism** with **Warm Tactile Luxury**:
- Generous breathing room that allows salon imagery and service menus to breathe without visual friction.
- Precise, rounded micro-interactions that feel soft, feminine, and deliberate.
- Uncompromising functional clarity, utilizing dedicated alert pigments (such as distinct crimson accents for unavailable slots) that provide intuitive operational feedback without disrupting the brand's regal harmony.

## Colors

The palette establishes an immediate sensation of elegance and poise through the interplay of deep imperial plum, vibrant royal violet, and gentle skincare-inspired neutrals:

- **Primary (`#5B1B7D`)**: Imperial Plum. Anchors top-level headers, primary brand callouts, active state indicators, and signature interactive buttons. Evokes opulence, trust, and luxury cosmetics.
- **Secondary (`#8A2BE2`)**: Blue Violet. Drives interactive vibrancy—used for active map pins, interactive tags, ratings badges, and focus rings.
- **Tertiary (`#E9D5FF`)**: Luminous Lavender. Deployed for subtle background fills, soft border boundaries, selection chips, and soft glow accents.
- **Neutral (`#FAF7F5`)**: Pure Alabaster / Cream. Serves as the canvas backdrop, trading harsh sterile whites for a warmer, skin-friendly ambient surface.
- **Named Colors**:
  - `status-unavailable` (`#D32F2F`): High-clarity crimson dedicated strictly to unavailable or booked time slots, cancellation warnings, and blocked calendar hours.
  - `status-available` (`#1B8755`): Subdued jade green for confirmed confirmations, open bookings, and available walk-in flags.
  - `surface-cream` (`#FFFDF9`): Elevated card and bottom sheet background to lift content cleanly over the page background.
  - `plum-deep` (`#3A0D52`): Used for primary high-contrast typography, giving texts softer depth than pure carbon black.
  - `lavender-subtle` (`#F4EEFB`): Softest tier surface tint for category chips and table list zebra-striping.

## Typography

The design system employs **Plus Jakarta Sans** as the unified typographic voice across display titles, body prose, and UI controls. With its modern geometric underpinnings, generous x-height, and subtly softened terminals, Plus Jakarta Sans effortlessly evokes both approachable Parisian fashion editorial design and razor-sharp mobile utility.

- **Headlines**: Set with bold weights (`700`) and tight tracking to command authority in discovery pages, venue names, and category headings.
- **Body copy**: Prioritizes open spacing and natural readability (`lineHeight` 1.45–1.5x) to accommodate extensive service breakdowns, therapist biographies, and customer testimonials.
- **Labels & Microcopy**: Medium to semi-bold weights (`600` and `700`) maintain crisp legibility in compact environments like slot selectors, map marker callouts, and duration badges.
- **Mobile Scalability**: Display headlines drop from 36px to 28px on mobile viewports to prevent cumbersome line wrapping while maintaining an impactful visual presence.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built on a responsive 4-column structure on mobile devices, transitioning to an 8-column layout on tablets and a 12-column system for desktop administration screens.

- **Mobile First Focus**: The interface reserves an outer margin of `1.25rem` (20px) to ensure thumb-friendly boundaries. Visual content carousels (such as salon stories and hair portfolios) peek outward with bleed margins to encourage horizontal flicking.
- **Vertical Rhythm**: Built upon an 8-point structural system, scaled down to 4-point micro-increments for compact interactive items (like pricing tags, rating stars, and status badges).
- **Sticky Actions**: Critical conversion zones (e.g., "Réserver ce soin", sticky date/time confirmations) rest within fixed bottom sheets padded with `space-md` plus device-safe bottom insets.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy relies on soft, atmospheric light diffusion combined with tonal layering rather than dense dropshadows:

- **Level 0 (Flat Ground)**: Base viewports tinted with Alabaster Cream (`#FAF7F5`).
- **Level 1 (Resting Cards & Service Rows)**: White/Crème surfaces (`#FFFDF9`) framed by faint lavender-plum hairline strokes (`rgba(91, 27, 125, 0.08)`) and ambient plum-tinted drop shadows: `box-shadow: 0 4px 20px -2px rgba(58, 13, 82, 0.05)`.
- **Level 2 (Interactive Flyouts & Floating Filters)**: Lifted elevated surfaces with `box-shadow: 0 10px 28px -4px rgba(58, 13, 82, 0.10)`.
- **Level 3 (Modals & Booking Drawer Sheets)**: Full bottom-sheet overlays backed by a 40% plum wash backdrop blur (`backdrop-filter: blur(8px); background: rgba(36, 16, 50, 0.40)`).
- **Interactive Calendar Slots**: Deepen on tap using an inner glow effect combined with an immediate border transformation.

## Shapes

The interface embraces a balanced **Rounded (`2`)** philosophy, reflecting the curvature and organic grace of the beauty ecosystem:

- **Standard Containers & Cards**: Defined with an 8px (`0.5rem`) base radius for internal components, expanding to 16px (`1rem`) on outer card boundaries.
- **Pill Exceptions**: Action chips, availability badges, category filters, and primary CTA buttons utilize pill-shaped geometries (full `9999px` radius) to convey softness and tactile approachability.
- **Calendar Time-Slot Blocks**: 12px squircle rounding to present a friendly, tap-ready touch target without wasting screen real estate.
- **Media Containers**: Salon cover banners and vertical story snippets use smooth 16px corner rounding, eliminating harsh angles to keep attention centered on the imagery.

## Components

### Buttons & CTAs
- **Primary CTA**: Solid Imperial Plum (`#5B1B7D`) with pure white text, pill-shaped, with a subtle internal top border highlight. On press, scales to `98%` with an elevated Violet glow.
- **Secondary CTA**: Clear background with a 1.5px border in Blue Violet (`#8A2BE2`) and matching violet label.
- **Floating Map Action**: Elevated circular button with Level 2 shadow housing the GPS compass or list-view toggle.

### Chips & Filter Pills
- **Category Filter Chips** (e.g., "Massage relaxant", "Onglerie Gel", "Coiffure Afro"): Pill shapes filled with subtle lavender (`#F4EEFB`), text in `#5B1B7D`. Active state inverts to `#5B1B7D` background with white text and a faint checkmark icon.

### Time-Slot Grid (Interactive Calendar)
- **Available Slot**: Light cream background with a 1px soft border in Lavender (`#E9D5FF`), typography in `#241032`.
- **Selected Slot**: Filled with `#8A2BE2` (Blue Violet) with white typography and a checkmark dot.
- **Unavailable Slot**: Distinct Crimson tone (`#D32F2F`) background at 8% opacity, bordered by `#D32F2F` at 30% opacity, with strikethrough text in `#D32F2F`. Visually unmistakable, preventing accidental booking friction.

### Salon & Spa Profile Cards
- **Card Anatomy**: Rounded 16px cream container, featuring a 4:3 hero carousel, instant bookmark button in top right, verified badge, rating tag (`★ 4.9`), distance indicator (e.g., `1.2 km`), and horizontal chips showing immediate opening availability.

### Form Inputs & Date Pickers
- **Text Inputs**: Minimalist pill-soft input boxes with `#FAF7F5` background, 1px border that transforms into `#8A2BE2` on focus with a 3px diffused lavender glow.
- **Checkboxes & Radios**: Custom circular selectors using the Imperial Plum fill with an elegant white check or inner dot.

### Badges & Status Indicators
- **Instant Booking Available**: Soft mint pill with a glowing green pulse dot.
- **Sold Out / Fully Booked**: Crimson text badge with muted reddish background.
